# Hero Section Half-Viewport & Circular Quick Links

## Overview
Re-engineered the homepage hero section to align with travel agency best practices:
1. **Dynamic Background Slideshow**: Implemented animated Ken Burns crossfading travel imagery showcasing global destinations and authentic travel vibes.
2. **Half-Viewport Height (`h-[52vh]` to `min-h-[460px]`)**: Compact, impactful hero presentation that leaves breathing room for content below the fold.
3. **Circular Quick-Navigation Hub**: Positioned overlapping the lower hero boundary, featuring circular thumbnail anchors with smooth hover elevation for Air Tickets, Visa Services, Passport, Holidays, and Travel Insurance.

## Navigation Integration
- Circular cards link directly to `/air-tickets`, `/visa`, `/passport`, `/holidays`, and `/travel-insurance`.
- Provides intuitive visual shortcuts for top conversion actions.
