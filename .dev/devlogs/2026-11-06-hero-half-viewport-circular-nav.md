# Devlog: Half-Height Hero & Circular Orbit Nav 🌄✈️

**Date:** November 6, 2026  
**Mood:** Adventurous  
**Topic:** Hero Layout Re-engineering & Circular Quick Links  

---

Hey all! We revamped the homepage hero to deliver that authentic, wandering-spirit travel vibe.

### The Big Shift 🎬
Instead of a static flat hero taking up standard full viewport height, we shaped it into a clean half-viewport hero (`min-h-[460px] lg:h-[52vh]`) featuring cross-fading destination landscapes with a smooth Ken Burns pan-and-zoom effect.

Directly underneath—overlapping the scenic backdrop—we brought in the circular quick navigation rail. Users can visually tap directly into Air Tickets, Visas, Passports, Holiday Packages, and Trip Insurance without digging through nested headers.

Clean, punchy, and fast! 🚀
