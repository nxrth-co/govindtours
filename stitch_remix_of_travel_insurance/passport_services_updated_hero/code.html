Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to get a passport?

expand_more

Standard applications usually take 15-30 days, while Tatkal applications can be processed in as little as 3-7 days after the police verification report is received.

Can I apply if my current address is different from my Aadhaar?

expand_more

Yes, you can provide other documents as proof of present address such as a registered rent agreement or utility bills in your name.

What if I lost my old passport?

expand_more

You'll need to file a Lost Passport application. This requires an FIR from the police station and additional documentation which our team can help you prepare.
