Pro-Tips for Embassy Filing

1

Interview Readiness

Be clear about your purpose of visit. Emphasize your ties to your home country to demonstrate your intent to return.

2

Document Sorting

Arrange your documents exactly in the order specified by the checklist. A chaotic file can lead to frustration and rejection.

3

Fresh Paperwork

Bank statements and NOCs should ideally be no older than 1 week at the time of your appointment.

4

Covering Letter

A well-drafted covering letter explaining your itinerary day-by-day acts as a roadmap for the visa officer.
