Common Questions

How long does it take? expand_more
Processing times vary by destination. E-visas typically take 3-5 business days, while sticker visas can take 2-4 weeks depending on the consulate's workload and seasonality.
What if my visa is rejected? expand_more
Don't panic. You will receive a refusal letter explaining the reasons. You can often appeal the decision or re-apply after addressing the missing information or concerns raised by the officer.
